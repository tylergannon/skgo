# Nullable in a form argument

correction: the 2026-09-25 finding (a Nullable on contact's Draft makes `go generate` fail on the generic origin) no longer reproduces on main. Generate succeeds and emits `nickname: string | null`; the failure moved to svelte-check, as an overload mismatch against `RemoteFormInput` in the generated `.remote.ts`.
finding: kit's `form<Input extends RemoteFormInput>` admits string, number, boolean, File, nested objects, arrays and undefined, never null; `convert_formdata` leaves an empty control out. A Nullable in a form argument is now refused at the authored field by `checkFormNulls`; `polytype.Optional` is the form shape (`?:`).
