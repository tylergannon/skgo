decision: `Process.Done` and `ExitError` describe the launcher, but `Stop` owns its exact process group. On Unix, continue observing the negative-PGID after the leader exits, escalate that group after the grace period, and serialize repeated/concurrent Stops. Go 1.27 `Cmd.Wait` can otherwise block on descendant-held `io.MultiWriter` pipes; apply a 250 ms default `WaitDelay` only when the caller left it unset. A real loopback worker fixture confirms the leader's exit status and tail survive pipe settlement while the owned listener closes after group Stop.
friction: The sandbox denied the default per-user Go build cache and unprivileged loopback binds -> use a task-local `GOCACHE` under `/tmp` and the approved ephemeral-loopback test escalation; no external network access was needed.
